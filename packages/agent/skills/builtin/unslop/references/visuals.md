# Visuals & Effects

## Decide what the visual is

1. **Does the screen need one?** Dashboards, settings and most app screens do not. Landing heroes and empty states usually do.
2. **Is there an image tool?** For a landing hero, generate a scene from the world as the background of the whole hero, with the subject on one side and calm space for the headline on the other. Then show the product rising from the bottom edge.
3. **Show the product.** Below the scene, or as the visual when there is no tool, build a preview in code of what the page already promises. Pick the one moment that proves the headline, not the whole app.
4. **Write the scene as one sentence.** Use the world and character from the direction and write the subject as one plain sentence before generating anything.

Avoid the AI defaults: no glossy blobs, orbs, nebulas, chrome liquid, floating cubes, neon circuits, holograms, glowing brains, robots, rockets or crystal shapes.
